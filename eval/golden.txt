# Golden set for `ytdigest eval run` / `ytdigest eval report` (issue #62).
#
# One video per line: an 11-character video ID or a YouTube URL (parsed by
# common.youtube_refs.parse_video_ref). Blank lines, lines starting with `#`
# and a trailing ` # note` are ignored. A leading `-` in an ID is valid.
# Duplicates are kept once (warning on stderr); at most 200 entries.
#
# Selection criteria (~20 episodes):
#   - a mix of transcript sources: youtube_manual, youtube_auto, whisper
#   - at least two speakers per episode
#   - lengths from under 30 minutes to 2 hours and more
#   - every video already ingested and transcribed (use `ytdigest run` first)
#
